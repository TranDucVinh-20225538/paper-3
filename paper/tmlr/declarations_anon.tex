\section*{Data Availability}

Both datasets used in this study are publicly available. The ISIC 2018 dataset is accessible through the ISIC Archive (\url{https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/}). The PAD-UFES-20 dataset is available at \url{https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zr7vgbcyr2}. The submission supplement contains fixed random seeds, the power-analysis script, derived CSV inputs used for the bootstrap-interval table and Figure~\ref{fig:power}, and reproduction instructions. The original 13-row checkpoint-level output matrix required to recompute the BCa endpoints, the full set of derived result tables, the extracted representations, and the per-sample distances are deposited in a public archive with a citable DOI; the record is not linked here because it identifies the authors, and the link will be added in the camera-ready version. Model checkpoints are available from the corresponding author on request.
