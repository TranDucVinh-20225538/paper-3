\vspace{0.25cm}
\small The success criterion this study pre-specified (Section~\ref{sec:stats}, $|\tau|\geq0.3$ \emph{and} significant at $\alpha=0.05$) lies below $\tau_{\mathrm{crit}}$ for all three designs, and is therefore unsatisfiable at these sample sizes: an association of exactly the pre-specified magnitude could not have been declared significant regardless of the data. Only the condition-number effect ($\tau=0.840$, the maximum attainable value at this design) clears $\tau_{\mathrm{crit}}$.
